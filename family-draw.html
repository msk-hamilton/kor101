우리 가족

u-ri ga-jok · My Family
How to do it
  1. Type your name.
  2. Draw a family member in each box. Your family can be real, made up, or a character’s family.
  3. Tap a Korean word under each drawing.
  4. Press Save my picture. Then upload it in Teams with + Add work.

잘했어요!

  1. Your picture was saved to your Downloads. On an iPad, press and hold the picture and choose Save to Photos.
  2. In the Teams assignment, press + Add work and upload the picture.
  3. Press Turn in.
